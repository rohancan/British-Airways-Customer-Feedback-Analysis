# British-Airways-Customer-Feedback-Analysis

**Project Overview**

This project analyzes customer feedback for British Airways to uncover trends in passenger satisfaction and identify key areas for improvement. The analysis follows the CRISP-DM approach, using Python for data processing and sentiment analysis, and Tableau for data visualization.

**CRISP-DM Approach**

Business Understanding - The goal is to analyze customer reviews and provide actionable insights to enhance customer experience and improve British Airways' service quality.

Data Understanding - Customer reviews were collected from an external source, containing details about passenger experiences, ratings, and sentiments.

Data Preparation - The dataset was cleaned to remove duplicates, missing values, and unnecessary text.
Sentiment labels (positive, neutral, negative) were assigned to each review.

Modeling & Analysis - Python (Pandas, NLTK, TextBlob) was used for sentiment analysis, word frequency distribution, and keyword extraction.
Tableau was used to create interactive visualizations, such as sentiment distribution, word clouds, and geographic trend analysis.

Evaluation & Key Findings - 60% of reviews were negative, with frequent complaints about delays, baggage issues, and poor customer service.
30% of reviews were positive, highlighting friendly staff, smooth check-in, and in-flight entertainment.
Seasonal trends indicated a surge in complaints during peak travel periods.

Deployment & Recommendations - Improve baggage handling efficiency and minimize flight delays.
Enhance customer service training for better complaint resolution.
Provide more transparent pricing to address concerns about hidden fees.
Upgrade in-flight seating and comfort to boost passenger satisfaction.
